Tobey Maguire portrayed the first modern live-action version of Peter Parker / Spider-Man on the big screen. He starred in director Sam Raimi's critically and commercially acclaimed Spider-Man Trilogy under Sony Pictures, making his version one of the most culturally iconic superhero iterations of all time.

Robert Downey Jr. portrayed Tony Stark / Iron Man in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU), a role that revolutionized the superhero genre and cemented his status as one of Hollywood's biggest superstars. Casting him in Iron Man (2002) was a historic gamble that launched the entire multi-billion dollar MCU.

Chadwick Boseman achieved massive international fame as King T'Challa / Black Panther, defining the role in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU) with unparalleled regal dignity, strength, and grace.

Mark Ruffalo portrays Bruce Banner / The Hulk in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU), bringing a distinct sense of gentle, brilliant vulnerability and weary charm to the dual character.

Tom Hiddleston portrays Loki, the God of Mischief, in the Marvel Cinematic Universe (MCU), delivering a masterclass in charismatic villainy, theatrical flair, and emotional depth.

Tobey Maguire has built a highly successful Hollywood career marked by a transition from indie darling to global blockbuster superstar, and later into an acclaimed producer. He is best known for portraying characters with a distinct sense of earnestness, quiet vulnerability, and youthful awkwardness.

Robert Downey Jr. has experienced one of the most legendary, high-stakes, and triumphant acting careers in Hollywood history. Widely considered one of the finest actors of his generation, his trajectory spans from a prodigious young talent to a rebellious industry outcast, and finally to an Oscar-winning global megastar.

Chadwick Boseman had a profound and deeply impactful acting career, specialized in portraying larger-than-life historical figures and iconic heroes with intense gravity, dignity, and quiet power. Though tragically cut short, his career cemented him as a master of biopics and one of the most culturally significant leading men of his generation.

Mark Ruffalo has forged a stellar, multi-decade career as one of Hollywood’s most respected and versatile actors. Best known for his incredible ability to portray relatable, deeply empathetic, and ordinary men caught in extraordinary situations.

Tom Hiddleston has built a highly distinguished acting career rooted in classical British theater and elevated by global blockbuster stardom. Celebrated for his exceptional range, intellectual charm, and intense physical expressiveness, he effortlessly moves between grand Shakespearean tragedies, sleek television thrillers, and iconic comic-book cinema.
